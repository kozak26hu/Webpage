🎯 Early-Bird Program

Referral Program

This is separate from the standing 5% token-level swap referral described in the whitepaper (Section 4.2). This program is specifically about growing pool liquidity during our current community funding round.

✅ What qualifies

You refer someone new, and they stake LP tokens (PHANDS/WETH) in our staking contract for the full 90-day LP lock period. Only LP staking counts — not the 30-day PHAND stake — because LP staking directly deepens our Uniswap pool.

💰 What you get

A one-time reward equal to 5% of the referred person's LP stake value, calculated on a maximum of $200 of that stake — so the maximum reward per referral is around $10 worth of PHAND, regardless of how much the referred person actually contributes. This keeps the program fair and predictable for everyone.

📊 Program-wide cap

This program has a fixed total budget of 5,000 PHAND, paid out on a first-come, first-served basis. Once that budget is exhausted, the program closes automatically — regardless of how much time is left in the funding round.

⚡ When you get paid

As soon as the referred person's stakeLP transaction is confirmed on-chain. You don't need to wait 90 days — the lock itself is already enforced by the contract, so we can pay the referral reward right away.

🔍 How it's verified

The person you refer should set your wallet address as their referrer using the "Set Referrer" field in our dApp (under the Stake tab) — this is recorded permanently on-chain. Then message us on Telegram with both wallet addresses. We verify the referral link and the LP stake on-chain via Etherscan before paying out.

Open dApp → Set Referrer

⏳ Time limit

This 5% early-bird rate is valid through the end of our current community funding round (see our Juicebox campaign). Terms for future rounds may differ and will be published here.

🚫 Why we don't pay for promotion upfront

We get asked regularly to pay for "promotion" or "marketing" with no way to verify results. This program is our answer: no upfront payment, no empty promises — just a transparent, fixed rate tied to something verifiable on-chain. If you can genuinely bring people who lock liquidity, this is how we compensate that.

🛡️ A note on abuse

Because the reward is a percentage of real, locked capital, farming this with multiple wallets means locking real money in each one for 90 days — a cost that generally outweighs the reward. We also reserve the right to review the funding source behind suspicious clusters of referrals and withhold payment where abuse is evident.

Ready to refer someone? Have them set your wallet as referrer in the dApp, then message us on Telegram with both addresses.
